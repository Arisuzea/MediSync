Sample patient privacy agreement

1. What we collect

Your name, student or employee ID, contact details, health information you give us (such as allergies and blood type), your appointments, and the answers you give in the symptom questionnaire.

2. Why

To book your visit, manage the clinic queue and keep your clinic record. Triage is queue-priority support only, not a diagnosis. Clinic staff always make the final decision.

3. Who can see it

Only clinic staff whose role needs it. Every access to your record is logged. Reports use totals only.

4. Your rights

You can see and download your data from your account, ask for corrections, and ask the clinic about how it is kept, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Sample staff access agreement

1. Purpose of access

This demonstration portal supports clinic staff in coordinating patient visits, maintaining sample records, and managing university clinic resources.

2. Confidentiality and appropriate use

Only access information needed for your assigned duties. Do not share credentials or patient information with unauthorized people. Verify the intended recipient before sending any message.

3. Clinical responsibility

Automated suggestions do not replace professional judgment. Clinic staff remain responsible for final priority decisions and emergency escalation.

4. Accountability

Record updates and staff actions may be logged for review. Follow your institution’s approved procedures for corrections and retention.
